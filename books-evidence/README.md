# Books & Evidence

Independent book-based research notes and source-checking guides by Falah Mousa.

**Purpose.** Use nonfiction books to identify important questions, then check claims against original publications, academic research, election observation reports and other primary sources. This is an original research resource, **not** a collection of Blinkist summaries, a substitute for the books, or an automatic fact-checking service.

## Research collections

- [Can an elected government weaken democratic institutions?](democracy-elections.md): comparative reading and an evidence check, with cases from Europe.
- [Reading and verification method](reading-methodology.md): how to separate an author's argument from independently verified facts.
- [Source bibliography](bibliography.csv): a machine-readable starting list. Publication details and evidence sources are labelled separately.
- [Book Research Helper](../tools/book-research-assistant/README.md): a small, optional command-line tool for looking up book metadata and preparing review worksheets.
