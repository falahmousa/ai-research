# Commission independent research and writing

**Falah Mousa | Research, analysis, writing and source verification**

I work with journalists, publishers, researchers, institutions and professional clients who need a clearly defined written assignment. Subject areas include AI and technology, culture, social issues, nonfiction books, international affairs and work involving Arabic-language sources.

## Possible assignments

**1. Source and claim review.** Check a defined public claim against its original source and relevant independent evidence. Typical deliverable: a dated claim assessment with links, relevant passages, conflicting findings and limitations.

**2. Comparative research brief.** Examine a specific question using several publications, institutional documents, datasets or reports. Typical deliverable: an English briefing, source index and a clear distinction between established findings and analysis.

**3. Arabic-to-English research pack.** Select and assess relevant Arabic materials around an agreed question. Typical deliverable: identified source passages, translations for research use, terminology notes and an English summary of findings. These are not certified translations.

**4. Article or editorial assignment.** Research, write or edit a factual article or analytical piece for a defined audience. Typical deliverable: an agreed draft length and reference list, with revisions specified in advance.

**5. Books and evidence review.** Compare the arguments of nonfiction books and identify what independent scholarship or original documents can substantiate. This is original analysis, not redistribution of a subscription service's book summaries.

## How an assignment begins

1. **Send a question.** State what you need to know, intended audience, available public documents and the deadline.
2. **Agree scope and price.** We determine source access, length, language, output format, fee and delivery schedule before starting.
3. **Receive the deliverable.** Findings are separated from analysis. Sources and limitations are visible. Unavailable records and unresolved disputes are identified.
