# Research guides

## Follow a political decision

Find the original announcement and date. Identify the responsible institution. Compare earlier statements and look for the document that puts the decision into effect. Separate a proposed change from an adopted decision and from evidence of implementation.

## Examine an economic claim

Find the original dataset and the period covered. Check the unit, geographical coverage, and whether the figure is a forecast or recorded result. Record revisions and compare like with like.

## Prepare an interview

List the interviewee's public commitments with source links. Identify unanswered questions, changing dates, or unexplained figures. Write a specific question for each gap and prepare a follow-up asking for supporting evidence.

## Four checks before using a source

1. Who published it?
2. When was it published, and when did the event happen?
3. What does it establish, and what is only attributed?
4. What remains unknown?
